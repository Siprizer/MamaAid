# MamaAid

MamaAid is a lightweight Android application designed to provide accessible maternal and child health information to mothers and caregivers.

The app offers:
- Pregnancy guidance from conception through childbirth
- Maternal nutrition recommendations based on WHO and Uganda Ministry of Health guidelines
- Child immunization schedules and vaccine information
- Frequently asked questions about pregnancy and child health
- Multilingual support (including Luganda)
- Simple, offline-friendly design for both rural and urban communities

## Purpose

MamaAid was developed to improve access to reliable maternal and child health information, particularly in areas where healthcare resources and internet access may be limited.

The goal is to present important health information in a simple, easy-to-understand format that can support mothers throughout pregnancy and early childhood.

## Features

### Mother Section
- Pregnancy nutrition guidance
- Trimester-specific health information
- Maternal health tips and recommendations

### Child Section
- Uganda Expanded Programme on Immunization (UNEPI) vaccine schedule
- Vaccine descriptions and benefits
- Child health and development information

### FAQ
- Common pregnancy questions
- Child immunization information
- General maternal and child health guidance

## Technology Stack

- Kotlin
- Jetpack Compose
- Android Studio
